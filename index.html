Voice-First Parking Companion

Expo SDK 55 Gemini AI Firebase RDW / NPR API

The Problem

European drivers face fragmented parking data, inconsistent pricing, and stale availability while on the move.

The Architecture

Voice-first Gemini intent parsing routed to a server-side aggregator unifying RDW, NPR, and OSM APIs with local-first caching under 2s latency.

Key Trade-offs

Chose Gemini over rule-based NLP for unpredictable voice patterns; local caching offsets per-query inference cost.
